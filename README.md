# Mini GPT From Scratch

![tests](https://github.com/S-Harshni/Mini-GPT-From-Scratch/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)

A small GPT written from scratch in PyTorch: a byte-pair tokenizer, causal multi-head self-attention, the training loop, and temperature, top-k and top-p sampling.

**Live demo:** https://s-harshni.github.io/Mini-GPT-From-Scratch/

Results and a full write-up are being added; run `python train.py` to train it (CPU) and `pytest -q` for the 9 tests.
